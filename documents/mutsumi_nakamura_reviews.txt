Discussions regarding Professor Mutsumi Nakamura on the r/ASU subreddit generally highlight her as a knowledgeable and well-regarded instructor, though students have noted specific aspects of her teaching style.

Summary of Student Feedback
* Teaching Quality: Many students have praised her as a "great teacher" who provides well-structured courses. In older discussions, she was frequently cited by students as one of the "best" professors they had at ASU, particularly for courses like CSE 230.
* Flexibility: A common theme in more recent feedback is that she can be "incredibly inflexible," particularly regarding policies such as scheduling tests or disability accommodations. 
* Course Difficulty: Students have mentioned that her classes can be challenging but often feature significant grading curves. Some students have noted that while the exams or tests could be difficult, the final grades were often better than expected due to these curves.

Professional Status
Mutsumi Nakamura is a Teaching Professor Emeritus in the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence (SCAI) at Arizona State University. Beyond her teaching role, she has been involved in various research areas, including artificial intelligence, logic, and cybersecurity-related studies.
